## Native shutdown contract

Independent review of the bundled 6.7.7 MD headers found `Release`, `Init`, `Join`, and `RegisterSpi`, with no `Stop`, bounded `Join`, or callback-drain contract. In the active-Join stop branch, the current adapter detaches the SPI and retains the API; `Release` occurs only after `Join` independently returns. If a vendor implementation requires `Release` to unblock `Join`, that could form a wait cycle, but this diagnostic receipt does not establish that as the cause. Do not move `Release` before `Join` based on this result; the vendor shutdown contract remains to be confirmed. See the [vendor contract questions](../CTP原生关闭厂商确认问题.md).
